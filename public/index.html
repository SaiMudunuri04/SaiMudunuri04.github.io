01 / The approach

Good models need
good systems.

My work connects model behavior to dependable software. I care about how a system fails, what evidence it returns, and whether another engineer can understand it later.

01 — DEFINE◇

Start at the boundary.

Explicit inputs, data validation, and leakage-aware evaluation create a trustworthy baseline before complexity grows.

02 — BUILD↗

Make decisions visible.

Documented architecture, inspectable code, and tests reveal why a model or retrieval system behaves as it does.

03 — OPERATE✳

Design for the handoff.

Health endpoints, deployment configuration, observability, and runbooks make the next step clear when things go wrong.
